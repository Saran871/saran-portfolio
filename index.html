About

I started my career in 2018 maintaining ERP screens in VB 6.0 and SQL Server, and moved into modern web development with React and Node.js. Since then I've built leave-management tools, video-consultation dashboards, travel booking flows and, most recently, a platform that collects and validates ESG data against GRI, SASB and CDP frameworks.

My strongest work is on the front end: dynamic forms, cascading dropdowns, schema validation with Zod, and state that stays predictable with Redux. I also write the APIs behind them in Express with JWT and PostgreSQL, and wire up secure sign-in with Azure MSAL.

I'm looking for a team where I can take on harder problems and keep growing as an engineer.
